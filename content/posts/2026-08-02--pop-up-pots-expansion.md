Hello Potters!

Summer's in full swing and it's been a busy one — Pop-Up Pots is popping up in more places than ever, we've welcomed some new faces to the team, and there's a class that could use a few more of you. Here's what's happening at Dover Pottery Studio, both inside the studio and out in the community.

## We're Back — With a Fresh New Site

It's been a while since our last news update here, and we owe you an explanation: we've been heads-down giving doverpottery.studio a full overhaul! The site has a new look, easier navigation, and a lot more going on behind the scenes to make finding classes, memberships, and Pop-Up Pots sessions simpler. We're excited to get back into a regular rhythm of sharing news here, so keep an eye on this space.

## Pop-Up Pots is Growing

{{< basic-image class="floatleft" src="/img/2025-11/pop-up-pots.png" alt="Pop-Up Pots logo" width="35%" border="false">}}

Pop-Up Pots — our on-location, single-session handbuilding workshops — has expanded to more partner venues across the Seacoast and up into Maine. Whether it's a cidery, a brewery, a farm, or a favorite local spot, we're bringing guided clay projects to more communities than ever, with new venues added regularly.

To make it easier to find (and get to!) an upcoming session, Pop-Up Pots now has its own dedicated page. Check out the full class list, a calendar view, and a brand new **Map** tab — a look at every partner venue currently hosting Pop-Up Pots sessions, so you can see what's happening near you and click straight through to sign up.

[Visit the Pop-Up Pots page](/page/pop-up-pots/) to explore what's coming up, and give our new [Pop-Up Pots Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/pop.up.pots/) a follow for the latest venue announcements and event photos.

## Welcome to the Team

Our Pop-Up Pots program has grown enough that it's got its own crew now — welcome to Jeff and Athena, who've joined us to help run operations and keep in touch with our partner venues and everyone who comes out to a session. If you're at a Pop-Up Pots event this season, say hello!

## New Open Studio Membership

We've also opened up a new, more accessible way to keep a regular practice going between classes: **Open Studio Membership**. It gives you studio access during our scheduled open studio hours, a shelf for in-progress work, and a lower monthly cost than a full Resident Membership. Take a look at the [Memberships page](/page/memberships/) for the details on both membership levels.
