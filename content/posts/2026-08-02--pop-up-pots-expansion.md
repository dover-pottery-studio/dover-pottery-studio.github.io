## Pop-Up Pots is Growing

Pop-Up Pots — our on-location, single-session handbuilding workshops — has expanded to more partner venues across the Seacoast and up into Maine this year. Whether it's a cidery, a brewery, a farm, or a favorite local spot, we're bringing guided clay projects to more communities than ever, with new venues added regularly.

To make it easier to find (and get to!) an upcoming session, Pop-Up Pots now has its own dedicated page:

{{< kilnfire_button url="https://doverpottery.studio/page/pop-up-pots/" text="Visit the Pop-Up Pots Page" >}}

There you'll find the full class list, a calendar view, and a brand new **Map** tab — a look at every partner venue currently hosting Pop-Up Pots sessions, so you can see what's happening near you at a glance and click straight through to sign up.
